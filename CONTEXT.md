# Kotoba

Kotoba connects teacher-authored Japanese vocabulary lessons with each learner's private review schedule.

## Language

**Account role**:
The durable capability assigned to a Google identity: teacher, student, or admin. Teacher and student accounts have one fixed workspace; an admin account may enter either workspace.
_Avoid_: Current role, selected role

**Workspace mode**:
The teacher or student persona currently used by an admin. It controls navigation, allowed operations, and visible domain data without changing the account role.
_Avoid_: Admin role, impersonation

**Admin**:
A development account that can switch between teacher and student workspace modes. It is not a third learning workspace and has no global product-management dashboard.
_Avoid_: Superuser, moderator

**Teacher workspace**:
The persona that owns classes, authors lessons and cards, sends invitations, and reads class progress.

**Student workspace**:
The persona that accepts invitations, joins classes, and owns card progress and review history.
