# Kotoba application

Mode: Operate. Teachers author and assign vocabulary; students accept invitations and complete focused reviews.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A calm digital study desk where one learning object owns attention; refuse dashboard-card clutter and ornamental “Japanese” clichés.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool mineral canvas, white paper-like surfaces, charcoal ink, vermilion product marks, and semantic red/amber/green answer controls. Large Japanese type meets compact, precise Vietnamese UI.

**STORY:** Sign in, enter the correct role workspace, create or receive a lesson, then move from a clear due count into a distraction-free review loop.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim brand rail frames a dominant live flashcard. On public entry, explanation sits left and the card sits right; inside study, the card centers at near-reading scale with four circular controls directly below.

**FORM:** Modern Japanese study planner grid, third in the grounded list; seed `c145e437`. Signature interaction: the card turns as one physical plane, then answer controls enter as the next deliberate state.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
